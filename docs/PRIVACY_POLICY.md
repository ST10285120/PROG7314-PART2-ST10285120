# FitQuest privacy policy

**Effective date:** 6 November 2026

FitQuest is a student project built for the PROG7314 module. This policy explains what the app collects and why.

## What we collect

- **Account details:** your name, email address and provider ID from Google or GitHub.
- **Workout data:** exercises, sets, weights, XP, streaks, badges and settings.
- **Photos:** a profile picture and progress photos, only if you choose to upload them.
- **Device token:** a Firebase token used to send you notifications.

Fingerprint and face data stay on your device. FitQuest never receives them.

## How we use it

We use this data to run your account, sync your workouts, calculate your progress and send notifications you have allowed. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Where it is stored

| Data | Service |
|---|---|
| Accounts and workouts | MongoDB Atlas |
| Photos | Microsoft Azure Blob Storage, in a private container |
| Notifications | Google Firebase Cloud Messaging |
| API hosting | Render |

Workouts are also stored on your phone so the app works offline. Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.

## Your choices

You can delete progress photos in the app, turn off notifications in your phone's settings, and sign out at any time. To delete your account and all linked data, go to Settings and choose Delete Account.

## Children

FitQuest is not intended for children under 13.
